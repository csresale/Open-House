OPEN HOUSE SIGN-IN: HOME SCREEN APP

What this is
A sign-in app for your iPad that works with no internet once it is installed.
Sign-ins are saved only on the iPad. Export the CSV after each open house.

Files (keep them together in one folder)
index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png

Step 1: put the files on a free website (GitHub Pages)
1. Make a free account at github.com and sign in.
2. Tap New repository. Name it open-house. Set it to Public. Create it.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file" and drag in ALL six files. Commit.
4. Go to Settings, then Pages. Under Branch, pick main and the root folder. Save.
5. Wait about a minute. Your web address will be
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/open-house/

Step 2: install it on the iPad
1. Open that address in Safari on the iPad. (Must be Safari.)
2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
3. Open it from the new icon. Let it load once with Wi-Fi so it can save itself for offline use.

Step 3: first open house
1. Tap Host (bottom right). Enter the address and a 4 digit PIN.
2. Tap Back to sign-in and hand the iPad to guests.
3. After the event: Host, enter PIN, tap Export CSV, then save to Files or send to yourself.

Keep guests in the app
Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access: turn on and set a passcode.
Open the app, then triple-click the top button to start. Triple-click again to exit.
Set Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock to Never during the event.

Good to know
- Sign-ins live in this iPad's app storage. Deleting the app or clearing Safari data erases them. Export first.
- The web address is public, but it holds no guest data. Guest info never leaves the iPad.
- To update the app later, replace the files in GitHub, then close and reopen it from the home screen.
